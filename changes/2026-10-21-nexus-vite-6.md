## 2026-10-21 - Nexus shell Vite development-server hardening

- **Security:** raise the Nexus shell's minimum Vite development dependency from
  5.4.0 to 6.4.4, incorporating upstream fixes that tighten transform access
  checks and safe-module path handling in the development server.
- **Resolved versions:** the shared pnpm lockfile resolves the `^6.4.4` manifest
  requirement to Vite 6.4.5 and its esbuild dependency to 0.25.12.
- **Scope:** the hardening applies when the Vite development server is used; this
  change does not identify a production application exploit.
