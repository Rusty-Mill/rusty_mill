## 2026-10-21 - rusty_hyper: the HTTP/2 transport (phase 4b-1)

- **Why:** phase 4b-1 of the first-party `prost`/`tonic` plan (card t45). `rusty_h2` has the protocol and a driver but no connection setup, TLS, timeouts or accept loop; `rusty_tonic`'s client and server (4b-2, 4b-3) need them. The design was approved before any code.
- **Added:** `crates/libs/net/rusty_hyper` (layer `libs`), everything behind the `rusty-tokio` feature so a default build is empty.
  - `connect(url, ConnectOptions) -> Connection`: `http://` is h2c with prior knowledge, `https://` is TLS with ALPN `h2` (a peer that does not negotiate it is `Error::AlpnNotH2`), optionally with a client certificate (`ClientIdentity`, using `new_with_client_identity_and_alpn`). It returns after a PING round trip, which shows the peer speaks HTTP/2, all within `connect_timeout` (default 10 s). `Keepalive { interval, timeout }` is off by default; an unanswered ping closes the connection. `Connection::{client, closed, close}`.
  - `serve(listener, tls, ServeOptions, handler) -> io::Result<Server>`: a closure `Fn(Request, Respond) -> Future` runs once per request on its own task. TLS must negotiate `h2` (using `new_with_client_auth_and_alpn` for mutual TLS). `handshake_timeout` (default 10 s) drops a client that stays silent, `max_connections` (default 1024) closes the next one unserved, and `Server::shutdown` stops accepting, sends GOAWAY and waits for the calls in flight.
  - One `Error` enum: `InvalidUrl`, `UnsupportedScheme`, `Io`, `Tls`, `AlpnNotH2`, `Timeout`, `H2`, `Aborted`. `rusty_http::Url` parses the URL.
- **Not included (by design):** HTTP/1.1, proxies, redirects, `Upgrade`, a `Service` trait.
- **Tests (written first; all failed on `todo!()`):** 24 over loopback TCP — `tests/plain.rs` (unary, trailers, empty body, 64 concurrent calls, a 2 MiB body each way, clean close, bad URL and scheme, refused connection), `tests/tls.rs` (TLS with ALPN, mutual TLS admitting a client and refusing one with no certificate or the wrong CA, `AlpnNotH2`, an untrusted server, h2c against TLS and TLS against h2c, a client offering no ALPN dropped), `tests/lifecycle.rs` (connect and handshake timeouts, keepalive closing a dead peer and leaving a healthy one alone, the connection cap and its release, shutdown finishing the call in flight and refusing new connections) and `tests/interop.rs` (the `h2` crate as client of our server and as server of our client). Timeouts are tested with a silent peer and short limits, not with sleeps on shared state.
- **Dev-dependencies:** `rcgen` and `rustls` (certificates and `CertificateDer`, as `rusty_tls`), and the `h2`, `http`, `bytes`, `tokio` oracles (never shipped). No TLS interop against a third-party TLS stack was added: that is covered by `rusty_tls`'s own tests, and a `tokio-rustls` dev-dependency was not justified.
- **Depends on:** #822 (`TlsAcceptor::new_with_client_auth_and_alpn`, `rusty_tls` 0.12.0).
