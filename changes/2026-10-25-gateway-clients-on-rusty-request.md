## 2026-10-25 - agentgateway: outbound calls on rusty_request

- **Changed:** `agentgateway-auth` (`extAuthz`), `agentgateway-llm` (providers, `promptGuard` moderation and webhooks) and `agentgateway-a2a` (agent cards) call out with `rusty_request` instead of `reqwest`, so none of the three depends on `reqwest` and `agentgateway-llm` no longer depends on `hyper` or `futures-util`. This is the first step of moving the gateway onto `rusty_axum` and `rusty_tokio`: `rusty_request` runs on either runtime. Timeouts, retries (a connect failure or a listed status) and the 32 MiB response cap behave as before. An `ai` route's timeout still covers a streamed answer to its last byte. An `extAuthz` denial stands once its status arrives: a body that is cut short, too large (over 64 KiB) or late is dropped rather than turning the denial into an unreachable authorizer that `failOpen` would allow.
- **Changed:** these clients take their proxy from the environment as `reqwest` did. `rusty_request`'s `ClientBuilder::proxy_from_env` now reads a proxy per scheme: `HTTP_PROXY` for `http://` requests, `HTTPS_PROXY` for `https://` ones (tunnelled with `CONNECT`) and `ALL_PROXY` as the fallback for both, with `NO_PROXY` as before. `HTTP_PROXY` alone no longer routes `https://` requests. A value without a scheme is taken as `http://`. One variable it cannot use fails the whole reading, with an error that names the variable but not its value.
- **Added:** `rusty_request` proxy URLs may carry credentials (`http://user:pass@host`), sent as Basic `Proxy-Authorization` on forwarded requests and on `CONNECT`, never to the origin.
- **Changed:** the gateway fails closed on a proxy setting it cannot use, such as an `https://` proxy URL: the route that needs the client fails to build at startup, rather than connecting directly.
- **Fixed:** a `rusty_http` header value with CR/LF is rejected without echoing the value, which is often a credential.
- **Known limitations:** a TLS connection to the proxy itself (an `https://` proxy URL) and SOCKS proxies are not supported, and `NO_PROXY` matches host names but not IP ranges. Requests now carry `rusty_request`'s `User-Agent`. Tests still use `reqwest` until they move with the server.
