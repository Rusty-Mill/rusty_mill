## 2026-11-16 - Mesh hub: the two-NAT test in CI (slice H7)

- **Added:** `interop/two_nat_test.sh cone|symmetric`: a hub on a public segment and one `ts-net` node behind each of two Linux NATs (network namespaces, `iptables` MASQUERADE, `--random` for symmetric). Root and Linux only. It fails unless: the receiver got every byte in order; cone NAT went direct within 10 s with at least half the bytes crossing the wan as direct UDP; symmetric NAT never went direct, almost no bytes crossed as direct UDP, and the transfer still completed over the relay. The run on this tree: cone direct after 2 s, 5.3 MB of 6 MB direct; symmetric relay only. Checked the other way too: with the NAT flavours swapped the script fails.
- **Added:** `ts-net` example `mesh_node` (receive or send a paced stream, report the path, check every byte against the sender's pattern; no hash, so no `rusty_sha2` dependency and its 1.98.1 floor).
- **Added:** CI job `two-nat-direct-path` (needs `plan` and `fmt`, in `required-gate`). The planner flag `mesh_nat` selects it when `ts-net`, `ts-hub`, `ts-daemon` or anything they depend on changes, or the script, workflow or planner changes; Linux runs only.
- **Hardened from the spike script:** unique namespace names per run and cleanup only of what the run started (the spike killed processes by pattern); no `wait || true`; bounded waits; automatic assertions instead of reading a log; no `git` call (it refuses a checkout another user owns when run under `sudo`).
- **Not covered:** full-cone mode; Windows nodes behind NAT; mixed pairs such as cone against symmetric.
