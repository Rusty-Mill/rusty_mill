## 2026-11-03 - rusty_orch: orch-lens, the lens runner's core

- **Added:** `orch-lens` (Let Em Loose #594), pure. The `Model` port asks one stateless question; `parse` checks every reply strictly (unknown fields refused, support needs executed or cited evidence, posts only where the job allows). `LensConfig` refuses a bad config with the field named: unknown providers, a cloud provider cleared for `sensitive`, a missing price, a rebel among the first two proposers, read-only tools on an HTTP provider, a diversity-group clash. Context assembly keeps the prefix byte-identical, hides foreign evidence on a blind pass and anything above the provider's clearance, and fits the window by dropping settled lines oldest first. The meter reserves integer micro-dollars before each call against the experiment, task and call ceilings. The scheduler enforces blind first, one look-harder re-ask, two proposers plus a rebel before attacks, no jobs on a sole candidate and fair attacks. ADR-0021.
- **Known limitations:** nothing calls it yet; the shell, adapter `Model` impls, persisted runner state, the breaker and lessons come in later PRs. A question is recognised by the `question` domain.
