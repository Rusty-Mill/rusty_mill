## 2026-10-20 - declared rust-version matches the dependencies; CI enforces it (issue #804)

- **Changed:** 46 workspace crates declared a `rust-version` below the highest one declared in their resolved dependency closure (default features, normal and build dependencies; an edition-2024 dependency counts as 1.85). They now declare that floor. Most move to 1.88 (`rusty_rand`, `rusty_win32`, `rusty_libc` and `icu_*` declare it): the `adk-*` family, `a2a-agent-server`, `agui-agent-server`, `mcp-tool-server`, `weather-agent`, `rusty-adk`, `rusty-acp` (was 1.86), `inventory-tauri`, eight `rusty_*` crates on `rusty_win32` and `rusty_confined_fs` (on `rusty_libc`); the `rusty-search-*` family moves to 1.85, 1.88 or 1.90 (`tantivy`); `inventory-cli` and `inventory-core` to 1.87; `rusty_fedora`, `rusty_opnsense`, `rusty_proxmox`, `rusty_sqlite` and `rusty_crypto_key` to 1.85; `rusty_rdp` to 1.75. Crates that inherited the workspace value now declare their own.
- **Not a behaviour change:** these crates already failed to build at the lower floor they advertised (checked for `rusty-acp` on 1.86 and `inventory-core` on 1.82), so the change corrects the declaration; consumers on a toolchain between the old and new floor most likely could not build them either (the other crates were not built at their old floor).
- **Policy:** `.github/scripts/check_rust_versions.py` runs in the `dependency policy` job on default-feature `cargo metadata`, and fails when a member declares less than its dependencies. It compares declarations; it does not build at the old toolchain. 9 unit tests cover it, and breaking each rule in turn (build deps, the edition rule, the comparison, patch levels) fails a test. Before this change it reported 46 crates; after, none.
- **Docs:** `rusty-acp`'s README, `CLAUDE.md`, `ARCHITECTURE.md` and `CONTRIBUTING.md` said 1.86 and that optional dependencies do not raise the floor; they now say 1.88 and why. `rusty_rdp`'s README says 1.75.
- **Judgement call:** `rusty-acp` documented 1.86 as deliberate and "verified in CI"; no job in this repository checks it, and the workspace resolves `icu_*` at 1.88, so it is raised too. Say so on the PR if you would rather keep 1.86 and pin the dependency instead.
- **Not changed:** crates that declare nothing; the dependencies' own floors (lowering `rusty_rand` or `rusty_win32` from 1.88 would lower the floor of more than 20 crates and is a separate decision).
