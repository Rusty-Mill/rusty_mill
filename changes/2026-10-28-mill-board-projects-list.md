## 2026-10-28 - Mill Board lists all projects in the side panel

- **Added:** a Projects section under Repositories lists every repository's projects with card counts. Choosing one
  shows only its cards and opens its repository with it chosen; "All repositories" clears it.
- **Known limitations:** a repository's own project (named like the repository) is not repeated there; choose the
  repository instead.
