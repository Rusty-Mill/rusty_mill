## 2026-11-15 - ts-net: EOF when the peer finishes sending (card #1466)

- **Fixed:** the stack kept a connection's read channel open after the peer's FIN until our own side also closed, so a reader's `read_to_end` never returned even though every byte had arrived. The stack now drops the channel once the socket is past the peer's FIN and everything it sent has been delivered, so the reader gets EOF after the last byte.
- **Not a data-loss bug:** the card says `shutdown` drops buffered data; it does not. `shutdown` already queues a close behind the unsent bytes and closes only when they are acknowledged. The symptom was the missing EOF. A 4 MiB transfer arrives whole and byte for byte in the new test.
- **Tested:** two stacks wired back to back: `write_all` of 4 MiB, `shutdown`, the writer kept alive, and the reader's `read_to_end` returns all of it (hung before the fix); a writer that finishes and drops while the reader has read nothing still delivers everything and then EOF.
