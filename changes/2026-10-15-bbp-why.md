## 2026-10-15 - BBP host: `bbp why`, and `policy` on `bbp card`

- **Added:** `bbp why --dir D (--task T | T) [--turn N]`. The log records who was granted a turn but not why, so the host recomputes it: fold the log to the event before each `turn_granted`, run `wake_set`, apply the policy's pick rule. Prints, per grant, the role, state, kind and `how`: the wake reason of an event-driven pick, `Fallback` when nothing was selectable, `DefaultHolder` for a turn-based task (with the shadow `would wake` set and its `agree`/`fallback`/`disagree` beside it, and whether `bbp shadow` kept the same record), `Explicit` for a forced grant (`Settlement`, `Consultation`, `ConsultationReturn`), or `Unexplained` when the replay does not reproduce the grant. Read-only.
- **Added:** `policy` on `bbp card` (`TurnBased` or `EventDriven`), flattened beside the core's card fields. `admin::card` is unchanged; `admin::card_view` is new.
- **Unchanged:** the engine, `event.rs`, `schema_for` and every log byte. Nothing is recorded; the reason is reconstructed.
- **Added (tests):** `tests/why.rs` replays the logs of two real event-driven runs and one real turn-based run (fixtures, with the turn-based run's shadow log) and checks that each reproduces the picks the run made; Explicit labelling on logs the engine writes (human `ask`, yield and its return, a settled request); the first of two selectable entries is the pick; and a guard that the restated pick rule never disagrees with the engine on either policy and on a roster with two servers. Mutation checked.
- **Known limitations:** the reason is reconstructed, not recorded, so a log replayed by a build with different wake rules can attribute a grant differently (`wake_rules` says which version replayed). The pick rule is restated in the host because the core's `pick_next` is crate-private.
