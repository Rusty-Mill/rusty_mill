## 2026-10-26 - agentgateway: the server is rusty_axum on rusty_tokio

- **Changed:** the gateway's listeners are `rusty_axum` servers and `main` is `#[rusty_tokio::main]` (#885). HTTP/1.1 and, over TLS, `h2` by ALPN are served on one port as before. TLS termination hands `rusty_tls`'s stream straight to the server, so `agentgateway-tls`'s tokio I/O bridge is gone. `agentgateway`, `-tls`, `-auth`, `-llm`, `-proxy` and `-mcp` no longer depend directly on `hyper`, `hyper-util`, `tower`, `tokio` or `tokio-util`, and `-a2a` drops `rusty_request`'s `tokio` feature. `tower-layer` and `tower-service` stay for the MCP handler until #886, and `tokio` stays in the build through `rusty-mcp`'s `tower` layers until then; nothing the gateway runs needs a tokio runtime.
- **Added:** `rusty_axum::serve_with(listener, accept, options, router)` and the `Accept` trait, which take each accepted socket before HTTP and return it plain, as a TLS stream, or as taken over (`Accepted::Done`). `serve` is `serve_with` with a fixed acceptor. The gateway picks a certificate by SNI there, and forwards a passthrough port's bytes without terminating them.
- **Added:** `rusty_axum::Admission`, which an `Accept` returns from `admission()`: at most `with_max_in_flight(n)` requests from head to response head, the rest refused at once with `503` and `Retry-After: 1`, and `with_timeout(d)` answering `504` to a request whose body and response are not done in time. It is checked when a request's head arrives, before its body is read, on HTTP/1.1 and h2; clones share one count. Middleware could not do this, since the body is read whole before routing.
- **Changed:** the gateway's `maxConcurrentRequests` and `requestTimeout` are applied as that admission, one shared by every port. A request now counts against the limit, and its timeout runs, from when its head arrives, so a slow or large upload is bounded by both, as it was with the hyper server.
- **Fixed:** on shutdown every port stops accepting at once, and then they drain. Draining one port before signalling the next left the others accepting behind a slow request.
- **Fixed:** `rusty_h2`: a server stream whose response is complete and whose request body the application dropped unread is reset with `NO_ERROR` (RFC 9113 §8.1), asking the client to stop sending. It was left half open, and a client that never finished its upload held graceful shutdown.
- **Changed:** every request body is read whole before routing, capped at the largest limit any route accepts. A route with a lower limit refuses past its own with its own `413`. A request head may be up to 64 KiB, a port serves at most 16384 connections at once, a keep-alive connection closes after 5 minutes idle, and a TLS handshake has 10 seconds.
- **Changed:** a response of known size up to 1 MiB is sent whole with its `Content-Length`. A larger one, or one of unknown size, streams as it arrives.
- **Removed:** cleartext ports speak HTTP/1.1 only. The hyper server accepted prior-knowledge h2c on them; `rusty_axum` serves `h2` over TLS ALPN only, as decided for it in #1080.
- **Known limitations:** response trailers are not sent, and a response header value that is not text is dropped (logged at debug). On HTTP/1.1 a client that hangs up while its request is being handled is noticed only when the response is written, so the handler runs on until it answers or its timeout fires; on h2 a reset stream drops the handler at once. A shed or timed-out request is not logged by the gateway. The integration tests keep tokio for their `reqwest`, `rmcp` and `axum` clients and mocks, and run the gateway on its own `rusty_tokio` thread. Moving them is a follow-up.
