## 2026-10-25 - rusty_axum speaks HTTP/2 over TLS

- **Added:** feature `http2` and `ServeOptions::http2` (off by default). A TLS connection that negotiates ALPN `h2` is served as HTTP/2 by `rusty_hyper::serve_connection`; every other connection stays HTTP/1.1, so one port serves both, as `agentgateway` needs to leave hyper (#885). Each stream is buffered into the same `Request` (peer address, `:authority` as `Host`, split cookies joined) within the same body and idle limits, and answered by the same `Router`. Streamed bodies (SSE) and HEAD behave as on HTTP/1.1; a client that resets the stream drops the handler; shutdown sends GOAWAY, ends streamed bodies cleanly and lets requests in flight finish.
- **Added:** the HTTP/1.1 limits carry over to HTTP/2. `max_head_bytes` is advertised as `SETTINGS_MAX_HEADER_LIST_SIZE`, and a stream whose header list or trailers decode past it is reset (`ENHANCE_YOUR_CALM`) while the connection carries on. `idle_timeout` bounds each body read, each write blocked on the client's flow-control window (the stream is reset), each socket write, flush or shutdown that makes no progress (the connection ends), and a connection with no open stream, counted from when its last stream ended (closed with GOAWAY). So a client that stops reading, at either level, cannot hold a response, its connection or a shutdown open. A connection has at most 100 streams open (`SETTINGS_MAX_CONCURRENT_STREAMS`). A request whose `Host` differs from `:authority`, or that repeats `Host`, is refused with 400.
- **Fixed:** `rusty_h2` bounds a header block's decoded size as HPACK decodes it, not only its compressed size, so a small block that repeats a large table entry cannot expand into memory. The cap is the advertised `SETTINGS_MAX_HEADER_LIST_SIZE`, else 64 KiB, never above 1 MiB; a block over it resets its stream, and the dynamic table stays in step.
- **Known limitations:** no cleartext h2c, no connection upgrades, request trailers are dropped. The acceptor must offer `h2` (usually with `http/1.1`) for the feature to do anything. The `http2` feature needs rustc 1.88 (rusty_hyper and rusty_h2); the default build keeps rusty_axum's 1.87.
