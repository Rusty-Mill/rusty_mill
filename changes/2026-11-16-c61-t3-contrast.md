## 2026-11-16 - C61 T3 contrast audit

- **Fixed:** CPU decorations ignored `minimum_contrast`; the GPU honoured it for fg-coloured ones only. Both now hold every decoration, including SGR 58 colours, to the ratio against the painted background.
- **Added:** `preset_contrast_audit` pins every built-in preset: text 4.0+ (4.5 except solarized-light, published palette), cursor 3.0+ (except catppuccin-latte), and `minimum_contrast` reaching its ratio on every palette surface.
- **Known limitations:** published palettes are not edited; chrome and gutter marks stay exempt (D76). Selection vs search precedence differs between CPU and GPU (not touched here).
