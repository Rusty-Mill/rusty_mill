## 2026-11-16 - rusty-mux client: find in scrollback (C55, part 2)

- **Added:** after the prefix, `/` opens a find bar on the focused block's last row. Type to search the history the client holds plus the screen, case-insensitively and across soft-wrapped rows; matches are reversed, the active one underlined, and the bar shows `n/total`. Enter or Ctrl-N goes to the next match, Ctrl-P to the previous, Backspace edits, Esc leaves and returns to the live screen. Keys typed while the bar is open never reach the block. No wire change; the search is the client's own (`rusty_mux_tui::find`), so `rusty_term_core` stays headless.
- **Known limitations:** literal text only (no regex or case toggle); history the client does not hold is not searched. A server-side `terminal.find` is not in this change.
