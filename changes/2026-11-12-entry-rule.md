## 2026-11-12 - Entry rule and project priorities

- **Changed:** AGENTS.md now requires a new idea to start as a remind-me design note. Cards or a repository follow only after an overlap check, and only for committed work. Work counts as committed when its project has a priority in `docs/projects/millwright/PRIORITIES.md`, which records Nano's 2026-11-12 tiers. Millwright G0, decisions entry-rule, backlog-commit and project-priorities.
- **Changed:** the Millwright decision log records the approved B0 triage of Ledger, Conflux and Swarm OS, and how their cards close.
