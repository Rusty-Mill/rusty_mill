## 2026-10-21 - rusty_h2: a reset after the peer's goodbye is a clean wind-down

- **Found by:** `rusty_h2::driver::dropping_every_client_handle_closes_the_connection_cleanly` failing on `windows-latest` (3 of 3 tries) with `Io(ConnectionAborted)`, on #822 (run 39575394412). It passed in #810 and on other runs, so it depends on timing.
- **Cause (what is known, and what is not):** the driver treated only `BrokenPipe`, `ConnectionReset` and `NotConnected` as "the peer is gone"; Windows also reports `ConnectionAborted`. Codex's test-only observer on #822 reported the failing operation as a **read** straight after a clean GOAWAY had been consumed. This PR's author did not reproduce it and the CI log alone gives only the final error. *Hypothesis, not established:* the client closes its socket with the server's SETTINGS still unread, which Windows turns into a reset or abort on the server. The fix stands on the terminal-state invariants below, not on that story.
- **Fixed:** `ConnectionAborted` is "the peer is gone". A read, write, flush or shutdown that fails that way is a clean end only in a clean terminal state, checked the same way on every path: the peer announced GOAWAY (or its input ended), **its input was whole** (no frame cut short, and no header block waiting for its CONTINUATION), and, where the driver would otherwise finish cleanly, no call is open and every frame carrying application data we sent has been flushed. Anything else still fails the connection: a reset without a goodbye, a cut frame, an unfinished header block, an open (accepted or not) call, an unflushed response, an unrelated error, an earlier failure.
- **Also tightened (a behaviour change on the existing kinds):** the write, flush and shutdown paths previously swallowed `BrokenPipe`/`ConnectionReset`/`NotConnected` after a GOAWAY or EOF even if the peer's input had been cut short or a header block was unfinished; they now require whole input too.
- **Tests (written first):** 14 deterministic unit tests over the in-crate `Stub` transport, which can now inject read, write, flush and shutdown errors and hold a flush (output accepted but not delivered). No sockets, sleeps or OS dependence. Each runs over the four peer-gone kinds. They exercise: clean end after a GOAWAY for a failing write, flush, shutdown and read; failure without any goodbye for write, flush and shutdown; a cut frame or unfinished header block after a goodbye on read, write, flush and shutdown; an open call (unaccepted, accepted); a response unsent with writes failing too; a response accepted by the transport but not flushed with **only** the read failing; a write or flush failing with a response undelivered; a reset after a delivered response. `tests/driver.rs`'s housekeeping test also covers `ConnectionAborted`. Mutation-checked: each condition above is dropped in turn and a test fails.
- **Not exercised:** a real Windows socket. Only Windows CI can show that this removes the failure there.
