## 2026-10-23 - ctutils 0.4.5 for RUSTSEC-2026-0385

- **Security:** `Cargo.lock` moves `ctutils` from 0.4.2 to 0.4.5, a patch release. 0.4.2 is flagged by RUSTSEC-2026-0385 (non-constant-time mask generation and `u128` comparisons), which failed `cargo-deny` on every PR. It is a transitive dependency through `digest` and `crypto-bigint`; no manifest changes.
- **Known limitations:** none.
