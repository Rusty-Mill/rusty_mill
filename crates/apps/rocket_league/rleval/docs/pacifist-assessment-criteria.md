# Pacifist System — Player Assessment Criteria

**Source:** *The Ultimate Rocket League 2v2 Guide — Achieve Success With The Pacifist System®*, AirCharged Gaming (First Edition Web Book, pacifist.ac).

This document enumerates the expressed and derived criteria for assessing a 2v2 player against The Pacifist System. It is the companion to [`pacifist-score-design.md`](./pacifist-score-design.md): where the design doc defines the analyzer's 8 scoring dimensions and leaves `TODO(you)` placeholders for "the precise definition + threshold for each from the book", this document *is* those definitions. Section 7 maps every criterion ID onto the design doc's dimensions with a measurability note, so the scoring team knows which criteria can back which `MetricExtractor`.

Faithfulness beats polish: where the guide is explicit the statement stays verbatim-close; where a criterion is inferred it is marked **Derived** and the inference is conservative. Thresholds are only ever the guide's own numbers — where none is given, the row says *none stated*.

## Legend

**Kind**

- **Expressed** — stated directly in the guide (statement is verbatim-close).
- **Derived** — a conservative inference from the guide's text, examples, or drills; not stated as a rule in so many words.

**Severity** (the guide's driving-test framing)

The guide classes fundamental breaks as **Minor** or **Major**, explicitly analogised to a driving test: *"you are allowed up to 15 Minor faults and can still pass. However, just one Major fault and it is an instant failure."* Many minors are survivable; one major should lose the play (concede a goal). The guide names examples of each but does not class every rule, so severities here are:

- **Major** — named by the guide as a Major-class break (instant failure).
- **Minor** — named by the guide as a Minor-class break (tolerable in small numbers).
- **Not stated** — a fundamental rule the guide does not explicitly class.
- **Guidance** — technique, strategy, role, or drill material outside the Minor/Major framework. All criteria in sections 2–6 are Guidance unless noted.

**Situations** (the guide's four main situations)

| Code | Situation | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| OD | Offensive Defence | Opponents have the ball in *their* half; we defend forward (full-map shadow, block the centre, funnel) |
| OO | Offensive Offence | We have the ball in *their* half; sustained pressure, pinning, boost starving |
| DD | Defensive Defence | Opponents have the ball in *our* half; funnel to corner, deep net, counterattack setup |
| DO | Defensive Offence | We have the ball in *our* half; break out without forcing, transition to attack |

`any` = the guide applies the criterion across situations or gives no restriction.

**Roles**

- **1st man** — the player engaging / applying pressure / nearest the play.
- **2nd man** — the guide explicitly defines the 2nd man as **the last man back, the player furthest from the action on their team** — *not* "second to touch the ball". Cover, deep net, central build-up.
- Roles flip constantly; they are per-moment properties (matching the design doc's derived-role model).

## 7. Mapping to the design doc's scoring dimensions

Dimensions per `pacifist-score-design.md` §2.2: **Commitment discipline (CD)**, **Positioning fit (PF)**, **Rotation soundness (RS)**, **Challenge timing (CT)**, **Shadow quality (SQ)**, **Shot selection (SS)**, **Boost economy (BE)**, **Over-extension penalty (OX)**. (The design doc also lists "Funnel discipline" as a principle; it is expressed here through PF/SQ rows tagged *funnel*.)

Measurability legend:

- **Direct** — computable from replay positions/velocities/boost/demolish/touch data alone.
- **Proxy** — approximable from replay data with assumptions (e.g. contact points inferred from relative poses; "control" inferred from touch sequences; intent inferred from movement).
- **Not measurable** — depends on intent, vision, camera, communication, or off-replay data (rank, training schedule); at best a weak proxy. Flag as low confidence per design doc §2.3.
