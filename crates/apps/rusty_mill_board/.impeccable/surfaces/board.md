# Surface: Mill Board web UI (board, roadmap, metrics, archive, decisions, settings)

Mode: operate

## Direction contract

THESIS: A press room's job board: every card is a numbered job ticket moving through stages, and the
owner sees the whole run at a glance. Refuses the category default of equal grey columns, a side
panel and tag-pill confetti.

OWN-WORLD: Cool white sheet ground, near-black ink, graphite top bar. Process inks do state work:
cyan = held by an agent / in work, yellow = queued, magenta = reserved for what waits on the owner
(gates, decisions), vermilion = blocked. Hairline rules, no boxes in boxes. Hanken Grotesk for UI;
Barlow Condensed 700 only for job numbers and counts, tabular.

STORY: The owner opens the board, reads the colour strip (where work sits), sees the magenta
"waits on you" count, approves or answers, then drills into a project via the chips.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Graphite top bar (brand, view tabs, quick add, identity, settings). Scope bar under
it: repository select, project chips with counts, agent chips (owner's choice 2026-11-05: chips
bar instead of a side panel). Colour control strip across the board top with a magenta
"waits on you" link at its right. Columns below; empty columns collapse to spines.

FORM: Assigned direction 7 of 7 (print-shop job board); seed:50aa0579b43510d8. Raises: magenta
reserved for one meaning (from the orienteering course colour); tabular figures and density in
metrics (from datamatics); fixed card scale on a shared baseline (from the botanical folio).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished.
