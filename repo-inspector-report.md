## Method

- `scripts/index_workspace_capabilities.sh` indexed **182 of 183** workspace
  crates into **11,499** capability-index rows. The missing crate,
  `skillopt-cli` (`crates/rusty_skillopt/crates/skillopt-cli`), is not a
  pruning bug: it's a thin `fn main()`-only binary with no `pub` items and
  no module-level doc comment, so it legitimately produces zero rows. The
  known nested-workspace double-tagging bug (fixed in v1.0.1) was checked
  for and not observed — e.g. `rusty_term_l13` (`crates/rusty_term/l13`)
  and `agentgateway-config` (`crates/rusty_agent_gateway/crates/
  agentgateway-config`) each appear once, under their own name only.
- `scripts/find_clusters.py` grouped those rows into **774** candidate
  clusters spanning ≥2 crates. The overwhelming majority are noise by
  design at this scale: a 138-crate `module: src/lib.rs` cluster every
  crate trivially joins, a 120-crate `fn: new` cluster, and 461 of the 774
  clusters have exactly 2 members under a fully generic name (`fn: get`,
  `fn: parse`, etc.). These are triaged as `coincidental-similarity` and
  not enumerated row-by-row, per the skill's own documented expectation of
  noise at this scale. This section covers the clusters with specific
  enough names to read as real candidates — both ones carried forward from
  the prior report (re-verified against current source, since two years'
  worth of PRs happened in between) and new ones the 88 newly-merged crates
  introduced. It is not exhaustive of all 774 clusters.
- `scripts/scan_workspace_sovereignty.sh` inventoried **153** distinct
  external (non-workspace, non-dev/build) dependencies across all 183
  crates. (ADR-0002's own text cites 162 unique external dependency names
  from its review; the ~9-name gap is methodology, not disagreement — this
  script excludes dev-only dependencies and dependencies also satisfied by
  a workspace member, which the ADR's count may not.) This section covers
  the highest-usage dependencies by crate count, every one with a
  `rusty_<thing>`-named internal candidate, and every one already named in
  the review's known-context notes (issues #118–121) — not all 153 rows.
