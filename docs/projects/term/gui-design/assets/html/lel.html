Let Em Loose

The tab that hosts Let Em Loose: full-app view, inbox, states. Decisions D37 to D41; no new colours.

L1 Full-app view, dark
The Let Em Loose tab (D37). Intent is read-only (D40). Stages are derived and show state as a glyph plus a word (D39). Claim status is a glyph plus a word: settled is slate, open is dashed, yours is the accent with a solid outline, contested is the accent dashed (D38). The accent falls back to verdigris (D35). No amber. One primary button per screen, and only on the confirm. Sample data.
